# Security policy

Email **hq@secria.me** with the issue, the affected specification or code, and
steps or a test that reproduce it. Do not open a public issue.

We acknowledge reports within 3 working days, keep you informed while we fix
the issue, and credit you unless you ask us not to. Please give us 90 days, or
until a fix is released if sooner, before disclosing publicly.

**Scope:** the specifications in `spec/`, the Go and TypeScript code, and any
difference between them.

**Safe harbour:** we take no legal action against anyone who reports a
vulnerability in good faith under this policy, does not access or modify
others' data, and does not degrade Secria's services.
